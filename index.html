Questions

Which apps can I add?

Messaging (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Messenger, Google Messages, Google Chat, Slack), calls (Google Meet, Zoom), email (Gmail, Outlook, Proton Mail), social (Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn), music (Spotify, YouTube Music) and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity). More are on the way.

Can I use two accounts of the same app?

Yes. Add the app again and put each copy in its own profile, or keep one Independent so it shares nothing with the others.

Does it collect my data?

No. SideStack has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you anywhere. The websites you use inside it work exactly as they do in a browser, under their own privacy policies.

What do I need?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, on an x64 or Arm PC. Websites are shown by Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows keeps up to date.

Is it made by WhatsApp, Google or the others?

No. SideStack is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the services it shows.

Privacy policy

Version 1 · effective 21 October 2026. This is the policy for the current version of the app; it only changes with an app update.

SideStack doesn't collect any data about you. It has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything it keeps (your apps, settings, notifications and sign-ins) stays on this PC. The apps you add are the companies' own websites: what they collect is up to them and covered by their own privacy policies.

In short

SideStack does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics or telemetry. Nothing you do in SideStack is sent to its developer.

By installing or using SideStack, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy policy and agree to it.

What SideStack keeps on your PC

To work, SideStack stores the following in your Windows user folder on this PC only: the apps you added and their settings, your profiles, notification history, the list of files you downloaded, the permissions you chose, and your alarms, timers and reminders.

Each app's browser data (its sign-in, cookies and cache) is kept the same way a web browser keeps it, in SideStack's folder on this PC.

Deleting an app or a profile in SideStack deletes its browser data. Uninstalling SideStack removes everything it stored. Files you downloaded stay in your Downloads folder.

The websites you use in SideStack

The apps in SideStack, such as WhatsApp, Gmail or Slack, are the companies' own websites, shown as they would be in a web browser. You sign in to them directly, and SideStack never sees or stores your passwords.

Those websites collect and use data under their own terms and privacy policies, not this one. SideStack has no control over what they collect and isn't responsible for it. Please read the policies of the services you use.

SideStack isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the companies whose websites it shows. Their names and logos belong to them and are used only to identify their services.

Permissions

A website can use your camera, microphone, screen, location or notifications only when it asks and you allow it. Location is blocked unless you allow it for an app. You can change or reset these choices at any time in Settings, and Windows' own privacy settings still apply.

Notifications, alarms and reminders

Notifications from your apps are shown through Windows and their history is kept on this PC. Alarms, timers and reminders are stored on this PC and ring through Windows notifications. None of them are sent anywhere.

Internet connections

SideStack connects only to the websites of the apps you add and the addresses they point it to: to show them, and to fetch their icons, album art and notification pictures.

Websites are shown by Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows kept up to date by Microsoft. Like Microsoft Edge, it can check addresses with Microsoft Defender SmartScreen to protect you from harmful websites, under Microsoft's privacy statement. SideStack's updates come through the Microsoft Store, also under Microsoft's terms.

Error reports

If something goes wrong, SideStack writes an error log on this PC. It is never sent automatically. You can choose to share it when asking for help.

Children

SideStack is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change in future versions of SideStack, for example if new features need it. The policy for the version you have installed never changes without an update: it is built into the app, and SideStack tells you when an update brings a new one.
